This is a bot I innitially made on my first year at Técnico to have all the announcements of my courses on Discord for me and my friends. The idea came from a similar bot they had in the IstHub (official Técnico community), but I implemented it in a webhook to save resources and only for the courses and majors of my friends.
Although this is a small project, I believe it can easily scalable to accomodate more courses and majores/channels. As such, if any IstHub (or other major communities of colleges that use Fenix) moderators are interested in this project, hit me up ;) (here or in my Discord, where I might answer faster: sillymamaco).

The first version of this bot (now deleted) used to scan RSS feeds to get the announcements but as the Fenix overlords decided we now need to be logged in for everything, including accessing those RSS feeds, i had to resort to a different approach with help of a friend.

It is required that whoever wants to repurpose this project for their own servers sets up their own .env (as per the example) and adjust the config.py to fit their needs :)
